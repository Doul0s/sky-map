Sky Map – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Sky Map ("the app") is a free Android app that shows the night sky in real time. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it. In short: the app does not collect, store, or share your personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Approximate location. The app asks for location permission so it can work out which stars, planets, the Sun and the Moon are above you at your position.
  • Device motion sensors. The app reads the accelerometer, magnetometer and rotation sensors to know where your phone is pointing. Sensor data needs no permission prompt.

How the data is used

Location and sensor readings are used only on your device, in memory, to calculate and draw the sky. They are not saved to storage, not sent to any server, and not shared with anyone. When you close the app, they are gone.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • No advertising.
  • No transmission of your data over the internet. Star and constellation data is bundled inside the app.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke the location permission at any time in your phone's settings (Settings > Apps > Sky Map > Permissions). Without location, the app cannot show an accurate sky for your position.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party services that receive your data. Downloading the app through Google Play is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
